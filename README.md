# Doubles Tennis Stats

A dead-simple, single-page phone web app for tracking doubles tennis stats live from the stands.
One tap per event, works offline, and keeps all data in the browser's localStorage. No accounts, no server, no tracking.

**Use:** open the page on your phone, tap Share, then **Add to Home Screen**. Pick a player at the top, then tap serve, error, and winner buttons as points happen. **Undo** fixes mistakes. **Stats** compares both players and suggests what to work on. **Matches** lets you start a new match and look back at old ones.

Files: `index.html` (the whole app), `manifest.webmanifest`, `sw.js` (offline cache), and the icons.
